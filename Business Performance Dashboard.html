Only cohorts whose first month falls within the selected period are shown, with follow-up through the end of that period, up to 12 months. Source: customer_analysis.xlsx / clean data · 6 Jan 2003 – 31 May 2005 · 2,815 product rows · 307 orders · 92 customer names.
Sales value is taken directly from the sales column; the currency is unspecified. Sales KPIs include only Shipped orders; the cancellation rate is Cancelled orders / all orders matching the filters.
Calculation rules & interpretation notes

Charts use order dates, not shipping dates. Resolved orders are excluded from shipped sales value. When filtering by product line, AOV = the sales value of rows in that line / the number of orders containing that line. The dataset has no customer IDs; customer names are used as identifiers.

Comparisons use the immediately preceding period with the same number of months and the same country and product line filters. “—” is shown if the comparison period falls outside the dataset or the previous value is zero. Cancellation rates are compared in percentage points. Historical data should not be interpreted as live data.

First-time customers are those first observed in this dataset. Contact and unit price columns have data quality issues and are excluded from this dashboard. Data is embedded in the HTML file; filtering and CSV export run in the browser without sending data over the internet.
